Quick setup check

Before the camera opens, set up your laptop and workout space so FitLah can clearly track your push-up or sit-up session.

Laptop on the floor

Keep it stable, screen open near 90 degrees, with the camera facing your full side profile.

Full body inside the frame

Leave space around your head, shoulders, hips, knees, and ankles so body tracking stays stable.

Clear the workout area

Use a flat surface, move loose items away, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or sharp discomfort.

Flow on the next screen

Start camera, choose push-up or sit-up, get into position, then your first valid rep starts the timer.

Safety note

Warm up first. For sit-ups, keep hands touching your ears. For push-ups, keep your body aligned and avoid placing the laptop where it can be kicked or stepped on.
