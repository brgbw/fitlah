Quick setup check

Before the camera opens, set up your laptop and workout space so FitLah can clearly track your push-up or sit-up session.

Step 1 of 4 Camera setup guide

Laptop on the floor

Keep your laptop stable on the floor with the screen opened around 90 degrees. Ensure the webcam captures your full side profile clearly.

Full body inside the frame

Leave space around your head, shoulders, hips, knees, and ankles so body tracking remains stable throughout the workout.

Clear the workout area

Use a flat surface and remove nearby objects that may block movement. Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or discomfort.

Ready for workout detection

Start the camera, choose your exercise mode, position yourself correctly, and your first valid rep will automatically begin the timer.

Safety note

Warm up before starting. For sit-ups, keep your hands touching your ears. For push-ups, maintain proper body alignment and avoid placing the laptop where it can be kicked or stepped on.
